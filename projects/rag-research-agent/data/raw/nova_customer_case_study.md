# Customer Case Study: Alder University Research Lab

Alder University Research Lab is a fictional customer used for product evaluation examples. The lab collects experiment notes, literature summaries, grant drafts, and meeting decisions in separate folders. Before adopting Nova Insight Systems, members often searched manually and were unsure which version of a note supported a statement in a report.

The lab created a private workspace and imported approximately twelve thousand text passages. Research assistants primarily use the platform to find prior decisions and to draft cited literature briefings. They value citations because an advisor can open the exact evidence passage instead of trusting a generated summary.

During the pilot, the lab observed that exact project codes were handled better after hybrid retrieval was enabled. Semantic vector search still helped when a researcher asked a paraphrased question. The team uses a candidate pool of ten chunks and presents the top three reranked chunks to the answer model.

The pilot also exposed a failure mode: the model occasionally cited a file name instead of a full chunk identifier. The citation guard rejected those answers until the model returned valid identifiers. The lab treats a rejected answer as preferable to an apparently confident answer with unverifiable evidence.

This case study does not report customer revenue, contract value, clinical results, or public stock information. Those subjects are outside the sample corpus.
