Nova Insight Systems is a fictional software company used as sample data for RAG testing.

The company builds AI workflow tools for small research teams, student labs, and early-stage startups. Its main product is a document intelligence platform that helps users search internal notes, summarize research material, and generate cited reports.

Nova Insight Systems has four core business functions:

1. Document ingestion: users can upload Markdown, TXT, PDF, and web-exported notes into a private workspace.
2. Semantic retrieval: the platform creates embeddings for uploaded documents and retrieves relevant passages for user questions.
3. Grounded answer generation: the system produces answers with citations that point back to original source chunks.
4. Evaluation and monitoring: the platform records retrieval quality, citation coverage, latency, and failure cases.

The company does not position itself as a general chatbot provider. Its main value is helping knowledge workers answer questions from trusted internal documents.

The target customers are research assistants, technical writers, university project teams, and small companies that need lightweight knowledge-base automation without maintaining a large engineering team.

The company's long-term roadmap includes hybrid retrieval, reranking, citation verification, permission-aware document access, and team-level analytics dashboards.
